Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

PoiseNaija

PoiseNaija is a name that represents the confidence, grace, and cultural pride found in Nigerian pageantry.

I chose this name because poise means carrying yourself with confidence, dignity, and elegance, which are the qualities pageantry helps people to build. "Naija" is the popular and proud nickname for Nigeria, so the name makes the focus of the website clear and friendly to every visitor.

Possible Domain Name: www.poisenaija.ng

Logo concept: A bold gold letter P with a small white spark, set inside a forest-green circular badge with a gold ring, paired with the name PoiseNaija.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of PoiseNaija is to provide information about Nigerian pageantry, its cultural importance, and the opportunities it creates for personal development.

The website will educate visitors about the history and value of pageantry, showcase talented contestants, highlight Nigerian culture, and explain how pageantry can develop confidence, leadership, communication, and self-expression.

Visitors will also be able to learn about pageant participation and use a contact form to send questions or share their interest.

Planned Website Pages

  • Home: Introduction to Nigerian pageantry and featured content.
  • About: Information about pageantry, its history, purpose, and cultural importance.
  • Spotlight: Contestant features, talents, achievements, and pageantry highlights.
  • Contact: A form where visitors can send questions or express interest.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent what visitors may want to know when they visit the website.

  • What is Nigerian pageantry, and why is it important to our culture?
  • How can I participate in a Nigerian pageant?
  • What qualities make someone a good pageant contestant?
  • How can pageantry help me build confidence and leadership skills?
  • Where can I learn about pageant events and successful contestants in Nigeria?
